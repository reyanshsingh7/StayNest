How StayNest works

  1. Search and compare

    Filter by city, locality, property type, rent and amenities to narrow down listings.

  2. Send a booking request

    Create a student account, pick a property and tell the owner when you would like to move in.

  3. Hear back from the owner

    The owner accepts or rejects your request. You can track the status from your dashboard.

A request is not a payment and does not guarantee accommodation. The owner makes the final decision.
