Prototype – demo launch

StayNest is a prototype built as a college project and is running here as a demo launch. It is not a live service.

This demo is hosted on Render’s free plan, so all data may be wiped at any time when the site restarts, goes to sleep or is redeployed. This includes accounts, property listings, booking requests and uploaded photos.

Please do not enter real personal information or a password you use elsewhere.
